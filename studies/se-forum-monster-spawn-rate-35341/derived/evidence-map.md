# Evidence map - SE forum monster spawn rate

This study records player observations from three posts in the official FFXIV
forum thread "Monster Spawn rate." All selected posts were published on
2012-01-14 and have no named patch marker.

The discussion is player-authored. It is not an official mechanics statement
or a retail capture. Every row is wiki-tier CALIBRATION evidence with a
`source-claim-only` verdict.

## Supported source claims

Jynx reports that monsters killed at different times could reappear together
and describes the observed cadence as a group clock of about three minutes.
Elexia independently describes a seemingly three-to-five-minute timer and
reports that some species can appear in place of another species. Jynx then
repeats that five mobs killed at different times reappeared together.

These reports support provisional investigation of a synchronized, real-time
group clock and shared-species slots. They do not establish exact timing or
complete slot topology.

## Claim limits

- The source never identifies Eorzean time or another accelerated world clock.
- "Every 3 minutes or so" and "seemingly 3-5 minute" are approximate player
  estimates, not an exact tick measurement.
- No post maps different intervals to a region, zone, camp, or monster family.
- The posts do not define group boundaries or say that every monster in a zone
  shares one clock.
- The two substitution examples do not establish all occupants, weights,
  ordering, vacancy behavior, or whether the substitutions share one location.
- The posts do not identify corpse-removal timing or the precise event from
  which the interval is measured.
- The thread has no named patch marker or controlled observation log.

## Verdict

Use this source to calibrate a provisional three-to-five-minute real-time
respawn window and to prioritize synchronized group-clock and shared-species
support. Do not promote Eorzean-time minutes, region-specific intervals, a
fixed three-minute rule, or inferred slot membership as retail-confirmed facts.
