# SE forum monster spawn-rate discussion

## Study contents

This study preserves player observations about ordinary monster respawn timing,
synchronized group respawns, and shared-species substitutions from the forum
thread "Monster Spawn rate."

The discussion is hosted on the official FFXIV forum, but its authors are
players. It is wiki-tier CALIBRATION evidence, not official mechanics
documentation or a retail packet capture.

## Start here

- `derived/evidence-map.md` explains the supported claims and limits.
- `derived/spawn-rate-claims.csv` contains the normalized claim ledger.

## Promoted conclusions

The posts support a provisional real-time respawn interval of roughly three to
five minutes and synchronized respawn behavior within observed monster groups.
One post also reports Dodo/Marmot and Desert Peiste/Antling substitutions.

The source does not establish Eorzean-time minutes, a precise server tick,
region-specific timing, exact group boundaries, complete candidate membership,
or a selection algorithm. Those details remain unresolved.
