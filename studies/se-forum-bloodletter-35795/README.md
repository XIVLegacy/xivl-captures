# SE forum Bloodletter claims

## Study contents

This study preserves four player-authored Bloodletter and Piety claims from
"STR/DEX/PIE/ATK Testing." It contains one selected source excerpt, one
normalized claim ledger, and a tiered comparison with existing retail records.

The discussion is hosted on the official FFXIV forum, but its authors are
players. It is wiki tier and CALIBRATION grade. Hosting does not make the claims
official Square Enix mechanics documentation.

## Start here

- `derived/evidence-map.md` explains corroboration and claim limits.
- `derived/bloodletter-claims.csv` contains the normalized source claims.

`sources/se-forum-bloodletter-35795/objects/pages/thread-35795-bloodletter-posts.md`

## Promoted conclusions

The claim ledger is suitable as a stable public provenance record. Existing
retail records identify Bloodletter and describe damage over time, a Gloom
Arrow combo, and qualitative damage when the effect ends. They do not establish
that PIE modifies Bloodletter damage or application chance. No formula or
numeric effect policy is promoted.

## Topics

- A stated PIE and damage-over-time relationship
- Player impressions about Bloodletter application reliability
- One confounded gear-change observation
- One approximate, single-fight application-rate estimate
- Retail action and status-description cross-checks

## Evidence gaps

- The selected posts have no named patch marker.
- No post supplies a controlled baseline or raw trial log.
- The gear-change observation changed PIE and DEX together.
- No source establishes an enfeebling-potency mechanism.
- No source establishes exact damage, status magnitude, or expiry damage.

## Further research

Use patch-scoped observations that vary PIE while holding the remaining actor,
target, equipment, and action state constant. Preserve these player claims as
calibration until controlled retail evidence reproduces or refutes them.
