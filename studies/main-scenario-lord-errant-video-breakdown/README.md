# Lord Errant - Sampled Video Observations

> Use the stable source-object citations below to locate this study's evidence.

## Study contents

This study records a small set of directly reviewed frames from the public
video [FFXIV - Lord Errant Quest](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tJrl9bfP5A8).
It is not a complete transcript. The footage shows a 1.x-style rank and
physical-level HUD, but does not identify the client patch.

## Start here

- [Evidence map](derived/evidence-map.md) records the observations and their
  limits.
- Source observation record (`sources/main-scenario-lord-errant-video-breakdown/objects/lord_errant_video_breakdown.md`)
  preserves the sampled frame notes.

## Evidence gaps

The sampled frames do not establish the full combat roster, Parley behavior,
combat outcome, quest-step transitions, rewards, or the exact retail patch.
These observations must not be treated as 1.23b-specific behavior.
