# FFXIV 1.x Lodestone Lore - GC Newsletters, The Last Word, Events

## Study contents

Clean Markdown transcription of the 1.x Lodestone **lore / flavor** pages - 63
pages, ~33,000 words, 152 images - harvested from the same January 2015 master
archive as the other lodestone sets. This is the lowest evidence tier: period
lore and in-world flavor, not mechanics. Grouped by kind in `derived/index.md`:

- **gc-newsletters** (30) - Grand Company in-world newsletters: The Mythril Eye
  (Immortal Flames, 11), The Raven (Twin Adder, 9), The Harbor Herald
  (Maelstrom, 10). Ordered by issue number per company.
- **the-last-word** (12) - The Last Word news serial, tracking the Garlean
  advance, Dalamud, and the road to the Calamity (Apr 2011 - Nov 2012).
- **seasonal-events** (21) - event announcements: Starlight, Valentione's,
  Hatching-tide, Heavensturn, Moonfire, All Saints', Foundation Day, Little
  Ladies', Firefall, Hunter's Moon.

Provides first-party source text for downstream lore and seasonal-event
reference. The verbatim SE text stays here in the evidence layer.

**Evidence tier: lowest.** Period flavor and story, not mechanics. Use it for
lore and color. It says nothing authoritative about 1.23b runtime behavior.

## Source material

- `sources/lodestone-lore/objects/pages/<slug>.md` - the 63 pages, transcribed verbatim. Short
  slugs: `mythril-eye-NN` / `raven-NN` / `harbor-herald-NN`, `last-word-<date>`,
  `<event>-<date>`. Prose ASCII-normalized; three loanword accents kept verbatim
  (facade, naive).
- `sources/lodestone-lore/objects/images/<slug>/*` - 152 images from the pages that carry them.
- Master archive: the full `Old Lodestone.rar` contains the same 208 pages and
  is not duplicated here.

## Promoted conclusions

The corpus is registered as first-party source text for downstream Grand
Company, Calamity, and seasonal-event coverage. No runtime behavior conclusion
is promoted from this flavor-tier study.

## Topics

- Grand Company politics and the alliance against the Empire (GC newsletters)
- The Garlean advance, the Mor Dhona base, Dalamud, the Seventh Umbral Era /
  Calamity buildup (The Last Word)
- 1.x seasonal events and their in-world framing

## Evidence gaps

- **Lore/flavor only.** No mechanical value. Do not promote anything here as a
  1.23b behavior fact.
- Event posts with actual battle content (Ifrit, Garuda, Good King Moggle Mog,
  Skirmish) are in the `lodestone-dev-patch` study, not here.
- Prose punctuation is normalized to ASCII. The byte exact original is the cold-stored
  HTML in the master rar.
