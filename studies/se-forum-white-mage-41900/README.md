# SE forum White Mage enhancing magic claims

## Study contents

This study preserves the Stoneskin, Protect, and Regen claims from post 1 of
"White Mage: A Guide." It contains one selected source excerpt, one normalized
claim ledger, and an interpretation of the source's repeating step sequences.

The source is a community guide hosted on the official FFXIV forum. It is wiki
tier and CALIBRATION grade. Hosting does not make the player's claims official
Square Enix mechanics documentation.

## Promoted conclusions

The claim ledger is suitable as a stable public provenance record. The source
supports step patterns and bounded observations, not exact continuous formulas.
No value is promoted to retail-confirmed behavior.

## Topics

- Stoneskin observations and stated uncertainty
- Protect defense and elemental resistance steps
- Regen base and AF Boots step sequences
- Regen's stated level 50 cap
- Patch 1.22c attribution

## Evidence gaps

- The guide does not publish raw trials or an exact Stoneskin formula.
- The displayed Stoneskin comparison does not unambiguously assign its values.
- The guide names AF Boots but does not independently bind that label to an
  item identifier.
- The guide discusses Protect, not a separately identified Protect II action.
