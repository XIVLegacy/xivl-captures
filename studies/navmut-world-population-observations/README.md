# Navmut World Population Observations

This indexed, provisional study preserves the pinned Navmut world-population
observation sets attributed to gavint130. The immutable source members contain
736 unique schema-v2 UUID records. The original JSONL contributes 650 records;
the later archive contributes 86 new NPC records and replaces none. Coverage is
numeric zones 128, 150, 159, 170, and 190. Zone 159 resolves to The Thousand
Maws of Toto-Rak. The study retains the existing Lower La Noscea and Mor Dhona
facets for zones 128 and 190, while exact client bindings remain unresolved for
zones 128, 150, 170, and 190.

Start with:

- derived/evidence-map.md for the evidence boundary and unresolved joins.
- derived/observations.csv for one deterministic row per source observation.
- derived/review-by-zone.md for the complete zone and category review surface.

The review categories are provisional candidates: NPC and misc records stay in
their source categories; Toto-Rak monsters and monster records with explicit
quest, Grand Company, GC, boss, or content notes are encounter candidates; only
remaining overworld monster records are ambient candidates. Raw labels, UUIDs,
notes, coordinates, map bounds, and rotations are retained. Zone 159 has the
supported client internal binding fst0Dungeon03. Other zone and subject
identities remain unresolved.

The additive archive contains only NPC observations. It extends the evidence
index and does not by itself authorize NPC scripting or runtime population.

These observations do not establish canonical population definitions, home
locations, slots, or respawn behavior.

## Promotion gaps

This study is not distilled. Promotion requires explicit evidence that is
currently missing: video URL, video title, video time ranges, source patch,
respawn timing, and confidence grading.
