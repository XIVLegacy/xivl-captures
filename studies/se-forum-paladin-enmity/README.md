## Study contents

This study preserves the Provoke, Rampart, and Sentinel enmity claims from post
1 of "Tachi's Guide to Paladin (post 1.22b)." It contains one selected source
excerpt and one normalized claim ledger.

The source is a community guide hosted on the official FFXIV forum. It is wiki
tier and CALIBRATION grade. Hosting does not make the player's claims official
Square Enix mechanics documentation.
