# Client Position Report 0x00CA Observations

This study records what the retail client sends in map-serverbound `0x00ca`
position reports, taken from every such report in
`move_around_gridania.pcapng` and `moving_around_gridania.pcapng`. The packet
is named `UpdatePlayerPositionPacket` in the Gridania scenario
[evidence map](../../catalog/scenarios/movement-mechanic-gridania-locomotion/evidence-map.md).
The field layout follows xivl-client-structs:manifests/c2s_payload_decoding.json
`c2s_0x00ca`. This study confirms that layout against the captures and adds
value statistics.

## Findings

- Every record is a 64-byte sub-event holding x, y, z, rotation as float32,
  a u16 moveState, and six varying reserved bytes.
- The moveState field takes only the values 0 and 2. Value 2 fills one
  unbroken run in each capture, 576 and 2378 reports. Value 0 appears in 24
  reports per capture, all outside the run. Position-changing reports carry
  value 2 in 573 of 574 cases in the first capture and 2374 of 2375 in the
  second. The one exception in each is the first idle report after the run.
- Rotation is the travel heading in radians. Over the 1456 steps of length 1.0
  or more, the rotation of the report that ends a step equals atan2(dx, dz) of
  that step with a circular resultant length of 0.9929 and a mean offset of
  -0.0023 radians. Heading 0 points along +z and positive headings turn toward
  +x.
- Mean horizontal speed over the 2378-report run is 4.92 units per second, a
  path of 2081.9 over a timestamp span of 423 seconds. The first capture gives
  4.83. Second buckets from the longer run have a median of 5.2467 and a
  maximum of 5.6313.

[Verdicts](derived/verdicts.md) holds every table and the statistic
definitions. [Accounting](derived/accounting.json) holds the same figures in
machine form.

## Limits

Timestamps have a resolution of one second. Only Gridania appears. Both
captures are on-foot play, so the data separates no walk, run, sprint, or mount
gait. No collision or terrain data is available. The mean speed lies near the
`0x00d0` on-foot baseline value 5.0 recorded in the
[movement speed band study](../movement-speed-band-observations/derived/observed-bands.md).
That is a proximity observation only.

## Not established

- Whether moveState 2 means walk, run, or another gait.
- The moveState values for any other gait or state.
- Behavior in any other zone.
- The unit of the speed values and whether they equal the `0x00d0` value.

Use `--check` to compare regenerated bytes and `--public-shape` to validate
retained products without the captures.
