# Evidence map - FFXIV 1.x Lodestone - A Beginner's Guide to Eorzea

The 12 "A Beginner's Guide to Eorzea" columns, transcribed into
`sources/lodestone-beginners/objects/pages/`. Per-column topics/order: `index.md`; provenance:
`file-inventory.csv`.

## Tier

First-party SE new-player tips documenting design **intent**, informally.

- Stronger than community wiki (SE's own columns), but the softest evidence in
  the Lodestone harvest: conversational Q&A, not a spec.
- Overlaps the manual set (`lodestone-manual`), which is the
  authoritative version of any mechanic these columns touch. Where they differ,
  prefer the manual; where either differs from 1.23b packets/client-data, those
  win.

## Highest-value columns

Remaining columns cover trading/items and further onboarding Q&A; see `index.md`.

## Gaps and caveats

- Informal tips; treat specific figures as the softest evidence and confirm
  against the manual set or the client.
- Prose ASCII-normalized (decorative circle bullets mapped to `-`); byte-exact
  original is the cold-stored HTML in the master rar.
