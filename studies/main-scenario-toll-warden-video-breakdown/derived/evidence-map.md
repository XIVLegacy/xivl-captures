# Evidence Map - Toll of the Warden Sampled Video

## Source and scope

The source is [Final Fantasy XIV Rank 30 Gridania/Main Story Mission - Toll of the Warden](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pwZtrzwEMVY), uploaded by RydiaMist on 2010-12-10. The video is 14:02. The source observation record is
`sources/main-scenario-toll-warden-video-breakdown/objects/toll_of_the_warden_video_breakdown.md`.

The sampled HUD is consistent with FFXIV 1.x. The footage predates the
1.23b period and does not establish client behavior for that patch.

## Observed from the video

- At 7:31, combat UI is visible with the nameplates `Amalj'aa Drubber` and
  `Amalj'aa Bowyer`. This is not a complete roster.
- The combat log is visible but too small in the reviewed frame to transcribe
  reliably. No action or damage claim is promoted.

## Creator-description claims

The creator's description places the mission route through Ul'dah, Gridania,
and Camp Drybone before a cave encounter and a return to Gridania. It reports a
three-person party cap counting NPC companions, and says that a player can
choose between Parley and combat. The author also says they did not know which
companion is retained for a two-player party. These are description-level
claims, not independently verified retail rules.

## Unverifiable

- The full enemy roster, party entry, combat sequence, Parley behavior, and
  reward are not established by the sampled frame.
- The creator's description is not a 1.23b capture or proof of 1.23b behavior.
- No journal, objective, reward, transition, or server rule was verified.

## Contradicted

No sampled frame directly contradicts the creator description. Unsampled
behavior remains unresolved rather than contradicted.
