> Use the stable source-object citations below to locate this study's evidence.

## Study contents

This study records a directly reviewed combat frame and creator-description
claims from [Final Fantasy XIV Rank 30 Gridania/Main Story Mission - Toll of
the Warden](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pwZtrzwEMVY). The footage is from
2010 and shows the 1.x interface; it does not establish 1.23b behavior.
