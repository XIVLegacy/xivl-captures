# Rivenroad Hard: historical guide and client text

Carraway's forum post preserves a Hard-specific Blue Garter guide. This study
records its reported phases, mechanics and literal wording as historical player
claims. The guide's Hard attribution does not transfer to quest-context or
repeatable-normal Rivenroad.

- [Evidence map](derived/evidence-map.md) owns provenance, text verdicts and limits.
- [Guide claims](derived/guide-claims.csv) preserves the selected mechanical assertions.
- [Client-text cross-check](derived/client-text-crosscheck.csv) records name candidates
  and seven quoted-reading classifications from the authenticated 1.23b CSVs.

The [source manifest](../../sources/se-forum-rivenroad-hard-51632/manifest.yaml)
pins the unchanged forum HTML. The linked video's gameplay was not inspected.
Neither the guide nor matching client text establishes authoritative AI,
thresholds, command selection or a normal/Hard resource assignment.
