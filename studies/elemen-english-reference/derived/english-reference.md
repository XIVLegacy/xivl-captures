# Selected English Reference

> Use the stable source-object citations below to locate this study's evidence.

This reference contains seven meaning-reviewed translations from seven archived
eLeMeN page passages. The raw Japanese pages and existing Japanese transcriptions
remain unchanged. The archive's evidence class is `archive` for preservation; the
translated content is secondary web evidence at the `web-tables` tier.

**Terminology:** English gameplay terms and proper-name forms below are translator
glosses unless explicitly cross-referenced to client data. The Grand Company
city/company forms use the verified names in
`xivl-client-data:docs/corpus-inventory.md:82-88`; no other client-data joins are
made here.

## Travel FAQ: teleport and return

**Source**

- Source set: `elemen-site-archive` (`archive`; content tier `web-tables`)
- URL: <http://elemen.sakura.ne.jp/ff14_dated_archives/etc/playguide/faq_aetheryte.html>
- Archive member: `faq_aetheryte.html` (`sources/elemen-site-archive/objects/ff14_dated_archives/etc/playguide/faq_aetheryte.html`)
- SHA-256: `8dea9c25ec9ffdb7c13a61ec046cbb3ac9fc609a2e848e9a35a4a24b85d0e300`
- Locator: `Q. テレポとデジョンの違いってなに？` and its following answer.

**Translation**

Teleport spends anima to send the player to a selected Aetheryte. If the player
is in a party, party members may travel along without spending anima. The
`デジョン` function returns the player to a registered Aetheryte or aethereal
gate. Registration is made from the landmark's menu. It costs no anima and has a
15-minute recast. If used while incapacitated, the recast does not apply, but
equipment durability decreases. While taking part in a Regional Leve or Behest,
the starting Aetheryte temporarily becomes the return destination; during an
Instanced Raid, the dungeon starting point does.

**Uncertainty:** The page is an eLeMeN explanation of final-1.x behavior. The
English spellings and menu wording are glosses; this translation does not verify
the mechanic against a client, packet, or retail execution.

## Guildleve FAQ: allowance stock

**Source**

- Source set: `elemen-site-archive` (`archive`; content tier `web-tables`)
- URL: <http://elemen.sakura.ne.jp/ff14_dated_archives/etc/playguide/faq_guildleve.html>
- Archive member: `faq_guildleve.html` (`sources/elemen-site-archive/objects/ff14_dated_archives/etc/playguide/faq_guildleve.html`)
- SHA-256: `36ff9cbd63efb520f30ba8a41150e89791e6cd8b245543d3b38ef161e0dbc554`
- Locator: `Q. ギルドリーヴの受注権ってなに？` and its following answer.

**Translation**

Four leve-quest allowances are granted every half day of Earth time, and up to
99 can be stored. The allowance pool is shared by Regional, Local, Faction, and
Company leves. A player may hold up to eight Regional Leve quests and eight
Local Leve quests at a time. The leve list at an issuing counter refreshes when
allowances are granted, a leve quest is completed, or one fails.

**Uncertainty:** This is the source's general FAQ summary. It is not a packet
census of allowance issuance or a validation of the historical server rules.

## Quest FAQ: journal retention

**Source**

- Source set: `elemen-site-archive` (`archive`; content tier `web-tables`)
- URL: <http://elemen.sakura.ne.jp/ff14_dated_archives/etc/playguide/faq_quest.html>
- Archive member: `faq_quest.html` (`sources/elemen-site-archive/objects/ff14_dated_archives/etc/playguide/faq_quest.html`)
- SHA-256: `96dd101899166a87773c509f72df900063fb2e0d4a11224ed58b86118c78e84c`
- Locator: `Q. ジャーナルについて` and its following answer.

**Translation**

Some journal entries, including quests with multiple scenario patterns, change
their text between the active and cleared states. Some entries, such as
linkpearl communications, can only be viewed while the quest is in progress;
their text disappears from the journal after completion. An active quest can
be abandoned from its journal details, except that a main quest cannot be
abandoned, and abandonment is unavailable during an event scene or while in an
instanced area. Speaking to the relevant NPC again lets the player resume an
abandoned quest.

**Uncertainty:** This translates the FAQ's described journal behavior; no
individual quest state or current service behavior is asserted.

## World guide: the age of adventurers

**Source**

- Source set: `elemen-site-archive` (`archive`; content tier `web-tables`)
- URL: <http://elemen.sakura.ne.jp/ff14_dated_archives/etc/playguide/world.html>
- Archive member: `world.html` (`sources/elemen-site-archive/objects/ff14_dated_archives/etc/playguide/world.html`)
- SHA-256: `642e6e6668060933f22e3a37dd3045506105bc1c8194952760db695aba4d9f5f`
- Locator: `冒険者の世紀`, from the paragraph beginning `十二神の内の一柱` through `その名は「冒険者」`.

**Translation**

The city-states of Eorzea, each devoted to one of the Twelve as its patron,
spent a long history in war, repeatedly forming and breaking alliances over
territory, interests, and interpretations of the gods. The situation changed
fifteen years ago, when Garlemald defeated Ala Mhigo, the strongest of the six
city-states. Faced with a common threat - airships in the sky and soldiers armed
with repeating firearms - the Eorzean cities secretly formed a military
alliance and entered a brief peaceful interval called the Age of Calm. In the
present day, the growth of regular armies left mercenaries and discharged
soldiers without work, and some became a source of unrest. A mercenary captain
concerned by this gathered capable people into a mutual-aid organization. It
established a new trade that takes on and resolves all manner of trouble: the
adventurer.

**Uncertainty:** This is setting prose from eLeMeN, not an independently
verified chronology. English place and group names are translator renderings,
not client-data label joins.

## History page: purpose and categories

**Source**

- Source set: `elemen-site-archive` (`archive`; content tier `web-tables`)
- URL: <http://elemen.sakura.ne.jp/ff14_dated_archives/etc/history/index.html>
- Archive member: `index.html` (`sources/elemen-site-archive/objects/ff14_dated_archives/etc/history/index.html`)
- SHA-256: `2848c9410602222574898866bc35826100146d32c9d46d5841133f708450b4cf`
- Locator: `ヒストリー` page introduction and `ヒストリー分類` entries for Monsters, Leve Quests, Quests, and Grand Company Rank Promotion.

**Translation**

The page describes conditions met while playing that appeared as History on
the official Lodestone site. A History with the same content was also shown in
the in-game log. The categories summarized here are: monster-related goals,
including defeat counts and notorious monsters; leve-quest goals, including
Regional and Local leves, maximum gil rewards, and Faction leves; completed
quests, including Grand Company quest categories; and Grand Company rank
promotions.

**Uncertainty:** This passage summarizes a historical tracker described by the
source. It does not assert that the external Lodestone still exists or that all
listed records were available for every character. The separate
`elemen-history-removed` study owns its 190 rows marked unobtainable; those rows
are outside this selected reference.

## Materia page: qualitative overmelding rules

**Source**

- Source set: `elemen-site-archive` (`archive`; content tier `web-tables`)
- URL: <http://elemen.sakura.ne.jp/ff14_dated_archives/gamecontents/Materia/index.html>
- Archive member: `index.html` (`sources/elemen-site-archive/objects/ff14_dated_archives/gamecontents/Materia/index.html`)
- SHA-256: `37ccb7d5da78f5101a2d18c166c6db5cec892be91ab0f7c1155a430adebf0a49`
- Locator: `禁断のマテリア装着`, including the paragraphs on the tool exchange, success-rate factors, and failure loss.

**Translation**

After melding materia eight times, the player can ask Mutamix in Thanalan
(X: 29, Y: 20) to exchange a Materia Melder for a Prototype Materia Melder II.
The latter enables the player to attach multiple materia to one piece of
equipment. The first attachment is guaranteed to succeed; the success rate
falls for the second and later attachments. The item's HQ status affects the
chance, stronger materia such as `マテリガ` have lower success rates, and the
type of materia already attached also changes the chance. The system calculates
the chance and displays it before crafting. If overmelding fails, the equipment,
the materia being attempted, any materia already attached to the equipment,
and the catalyst are all lost.

**Uncertainty:** The source gives qualitative factors but no numerical
probability table. The numeric materia value grid is client-redundant and is
already excluded from the web-table harvest. Translated item and NPC names are
glosses, not verified English client labels.

## Grand Company page: purpose and joining

**Source**

- Source set: `elemen-site-archive` (`archive`; content tier `web-tables`)
- URL: <http://elemen.sakura.ne.jp/ff14_dated_archives/gamecontents/TheGrandCompanies/index.html>
- Archive member: `index.html` (`sources/elemen-site-archive/objects/ff14_dated_archives/gamecontents/TheGrandCompanies/index.html`)
- SHA-256: `3bf4fe202978d7cc8d127c8e5e47480e1c726eeda7fe2525d60d56a6be2915c7`
- Locator: `グランドカンパニーについて`, paragraphs before the faction table.

**Translation**

A Grand Company is an organization established by Eorzea's city-states to face
the crisis threatening the world. The page names three: `Maelstrom` in Limsa
Lominsa, `Order of the Twin Adder` in Gridania, and `Immortal Flames` in Ul'dah.
These city/company forms follow the verified client-data names in
`xivl-client-data:docs/corpus-inventory.md:82-88`; the company's office can be
reached from each city's market entrances and streets. Completing a Grand
Company quest offered by an office NPC qualifies the player to join. To accept
the temporary-assignment quest, the player's current Fighter or Sorcerer class
must be level 22 or above.

**Uncertainty:** The English faction forms are client-data cross-references, not
translations of a server-side packet field. The level and class-family
requirement is eLeMeN prose; client-data eligibility documentation records the
same discipline-family distinction at
`xivl-client-data:docs/quest-gating-findings.md:50-55`, but neither source
independently checks server-side affiliation rules.
