# English Reference Coverage

This reference contains seven selected eLeMeN prose passages. It is not a
translation of all eLeMeN prose, mechanics, or archive material.

## Included passages

Seven passages were translated in [`english-reference.md`](english-reference.md):

1. Playguide travel FAQ: `faq_aetheryte.html`, teleport and return answer.
2. Playguide guildleve FAQ: `faq_guildleve.html`, allowance-stock answer.
3. Playguide quest FAQ: `faq_quest.html`, journal-retention answer.
4. Playguide lore: `world.html`, `冒険者の世紀` passage.
5. History overview: `etc/history/index.html`, page purpose and four category summaries.
6. Materia prose: `gamecontents/Materia/index.html`, qualitative overmelding rules.
7. Grand Company prose: `gamecontents/TheGrandCompanies/index.html`, purpose and joining paragraphs.

Each included passage has its source URL, archive member, SHA-256, section
locator, translation, and uncertainty note in the reference.

## Other material outside this reference

- `elemen-zone-guide` already normalizes weather behavior, teleport costs,
  adjacency, service-NPC and shop-NPC placements, and sub-map patch dates. Its
  client-primary map geometry and inventories are intentionally not duplicated.
- `elemen-instanced-content-entry-rules` already contains the 15 normalized
  entry-gate rows. This reference does not translate every source page or the
  separate instance-operation prose.
- `elemen-battle-actions` already covers seven battle-class action pages, the
  shared battle-tactics page, 226 normalized action rows, part-damage mapping,
  and six Battle Regimen effects. Its client-backed action names and descriptions
  remain in that study; older action snapshots and crafter /
  gatherer pages remain outside that study's stated scope.
- `elemen-history-removed` owns the 190 history rows marked unobtainable. Other
  history table rows overlap the client achievement system or are outside that
  study's filter and are outside this reference.
- The Materia numeric value grid and compatibility matrix are client-redundant;
  only the selected qualitative overmelding explanation is translated. No
  numerical success rates are supplied by that prose.
- Grand Company rank thresholds, ranks, seal-shop rows, and quest walkthroughs
  are already assigned to client data or other studies. This reference includes
  only the introductory prose and joining requirement.
- The site archive contains 271 preserved files across multiple sections; other
  prose passages in those sections are outside this reference.

These are scope boundaries, not claims that the material is unavailable or
fully translated. Existing study READMEs and evidence maps remain the
canonical inventories for their normalized records and gaps.
