# eLeMeN FF14 1.x English Reference - Selected Web-Unique Prose

This study contains English translations of seven selected eLeMeN prose passages.
It is a selected reference, not a translation of the full site or playguide.
The source Japanese transcriptions and raw archive pages remain unchanged.

Each translated passage records its source set, exact page URL, archive member,
SHA-256, and Japanese section locator. Uncertainty and coverage limits are stated
with the passages. The Grand Company city/company names use the verified client
forms recorded in `xivl-client-data:docs/corpus-inventory.md:82-88`; other English
gameplay terms and proper-name forms remain translator glosses, and no other
client-data joins are used.

## Evidence boundary

The archive preserves final-1.x eLeMeN content as a secondary web source. Its
prose describes the site's historical account of game behavior and setting; it
does not independently establish live retail behavior. Structured facts already
normalized by existing eLeMeN studies are inventoried rather than duplicated.
