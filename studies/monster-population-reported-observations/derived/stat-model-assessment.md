# Profile stat evidence limits

The [profile rows](profiles.csv) preserve supplied HP, MP and other stat values.
They are unverified source values, not retail stat measurements or a recovered
retail formula. Their source identities and field limits are recorded in the
[evidence map](evidence-map.md) and the
[source manifest](../../../sources/monster-population-reported-observations/manifest.yaml).

No identified retail measurement series, video locators, fitting residuals or
independent validation dataset supports a stat formula here. Profile values
do not establish retail scaling, resource precedence or eligibility rules.

Retail corroboration requires subject identity, level, observed resource or
stat values, conditions, patch or capture identity, and reproducible source
locators. Until those inputs exist, the retained profile values remain
historical supplied data only.
